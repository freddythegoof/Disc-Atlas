Hyzer the Disc · character sheet

Part 1 of the mascot brief: the look only, nothing wired. Each block shows the coach corner at real CSS sizes, in Paper and Dusk, then the seven moods the coach would use at medium (64px, live in the panel heading) and small (32px). The launcher shows a 28px still frame, not a live mascot, so the atlas pays no frame cost while the coach is closed. Pick a take.

Take A · cool, a little cocky

Steeper hyzer (24°), smaller eyes, one raised lime brow, a smirk set off-centre. Snappy, bird-like glances that hold; small hops.

Take B · cute and friendly

Gentler hyzer (17°), big eyes, blush, a centred smile. Eased glances, bouncy hops, more squash.

Live mascots: Wobnimo 0.2.0 (MIT), one shared WebGL2 context. Hover a medium Hyzer and he follows the pointer; click him for the poke reaction. With reduced motion turned on, every Hyzer holds a still pose.
