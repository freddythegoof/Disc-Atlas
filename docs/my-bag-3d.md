# My Bag · 3D bag

The SVG bag graphic is replaced by the ChatGPT-built 3D backpack (`floating-bag-web.zip`, three.js 0.180). The list view, including its inline pocket dropdowns, is unchanged. All changes are left uncommitted: nothing was pushed, deployed or migrated, and no schema or API change was needed.

## Integration

- **Direct module, no iframe.** `public/bag-scene.js` keeps the `BagScene` contract that `my-bag.js` already used (`update`, `reveal`, `setOpen`, `reset`) and drives `mountBag()` from `public/bag3d/viewer.mjs` directly. Every saved change (pocket, go-to, disc color, Storage, bag color, capacities) reaches the 3D scene on the same render pass as the list. There is no postMessage and no reload.
- **Lazy loading.** `web/index.html` declares an import map only. three.js, the viewer and `charcoal-bag.glb` (2.06 MB total, uncompressed) load on the first `reveal()`, when a signed-in user opens the Bag tab. Signed-out visitors and the map/list never request anything under `/bag3d/`.
- **Vendored viewer.** `public/bag3d/` is the zip's runtime (no demo page). The original API still works unchanged (12-ID disc API, URL parsing, orbit). The Disc Atlas additions are opt-in options, documented in `public/bag3d/README.md`. `bag-layout.mjs` holds the slot math with no three.js dependency.
- **Accessibility layer.** The canvas is decorative (`aria-hidden`). A positioned hit layer above it mirrors every slot, using the SVG's structure: `[data-pocket] > [data-physical-disc]` with the same labels, `data-slot-order`, Tab/Escape/Enter handling, hover/focus lift and touch double-tap. Main slots are non-overlapping columns. Pocketed discs are domes clipped at the pocket mouth, so the front disc wins where pockets overlap.

## Feature parity

| SVG bag | 3D bag |
| --- | --- |
| Go-to: accent rim, dashed hollow when empty | The front pocket, directly above the main compartment, like a real Grip-style bag. Since Oct 5 the go-to renders clean: no accent rim, outline or glow (the GLB rim stays hidden). The model's dashed outline still marks an empty go-to pocket. Several go-to copies stack in the pocket. |
| Main / putter pockets, capacities | Main slots = `main_capacity + extra_capacity` across the main compartment (thinner discs as capacity grows). Putter slots = `putter_capacity` in the top pocket, in a fanned stack so every rim shows. Empty slots are faint ghosts. More than 48 main / 8 putter / 6 go-to slots go to the existing "beyond the illustrated slots" note. All curated models fit. |
| Per-disc colors, bag color | Each disc's own material. The fabric follows `bag_color` with tonal panels and piping. Zippers and hardware stay fixed. The default charcoal restores the model's fabric. Tone mapping is Khronos PBR Neutral, so a lifted `#ed7868` renders `#f17f78`. ACES rendered it pastel `#f2b8b2`. |
| Capacity meter, Storage | Unchanged (they live outside the graphic). The 3D layout drops stored discs and re-adds them live. |
| Open/close | The model's authored flap fold, run at 0.75 s on wall-clock time (the SVG sequence was about 0.8 s). Toggle button, clicking the bag body, and reduced motion (instant) all work. |
| Hover/focus lift + info card | Since Oct 5, hover and focus show a dark name pill above the disc and light that one disc up in its own color; the disc does not move. The former info card stays as the visually hidden `aria-describedby` description (plastic, wear, weight, pocket, notes). |
| Size S / M / L | Since Oct 5 one size: 680 px (between the former M 560 and L 880), capped to the viewport height (`(100dvh − 150px) × 0.8`). No size control: zoom instead (+ / − / reset buttons on the canvas, pinch, or Ctrl/⌘ + scroll). The canvas re-renders at its size instead of being upscaled. |

Deliberate differences:

- With the flap closed, putters and go-to discs stay focusable and liftable, because they sit in outside pockets. Main discs are unreachable until the bag opens, as before.
- A sideways drag (mouse or finger) turns the bag, and it stays exactly where the user lets go: no ease back (fixed Oct 5). Hit targets and labels re-measure for the turn. Turned so the front faces away, only the top pocket's putters stay in reach. There is no orbit. Zoom (Oct 5) uses its own buttons, a two-finger pinch or Ctrl/⌘ + scroll; a plain scroll over the bag still scrolls the page (`touch-action: pan-y` on the whole canvas). OrbitControls never loads.
- The SVG asset `public/bag.svg` is deleted (nothing references it). Restore it with `git checkout -- public/bag.svg` if wanted.

## Slide-out, details and top view (Oct 4–5 2026)

- **Hover = name only.** Hovering or focusing a disc shows a dark rounded pill with its name in white, in every theme. The disc stays in its pocket. On touch there is no hover: a tap is a click.
- **Click = slide out along the pocket.** Each slot has a `slide` pose in `bag-layout.mjs`. A putter rises straight up until its whole face clears the top pocket's mouth, then steps forward past the front putter, so nothing in the pocket covers it. The go-to rises out of the front pocket and comes slightly forward. A main disc comes forward out of the open compartment, then up, turning face-on. The motion is time-linear progress mapped through two eased stages (along the pocket first, then the rest), about 0.6 s. Since Oct 6 the disc then moves on to a staged spot and stays out until it is clicked again. See "Out discs and map mode" below.
- **Putters in one row.** The four putters share one x and one tilt, with even spacing and a 16 mm step up per disc. The step keeps each rim visible and gives back putters a target of about 9 px tall at 360 px.
- **Details on My Bag.** Since Oct 5 the same click (or tap, or Enter) that slides a disc out also opens the atlas's own `#detail` panel, which takes focus. Since Oct 6 a second click on the disc puts it back (closing its details if they show it), and clicking an out disc's name reopens its details. The panel is docked on the My Bag page: the disc details plus a "Your disc" section with this copy's pocket, plastic, weight, wear, stability note and personal notes, Edit disc, and **Show on Atlas**. It never goes to the Directory. The list's disc names open the same panel.
- **Show on Atlas** goes to the map, clears any filter that hides the disc, zooms (4× or more) until the disc has its own marker, centers it where the panel leaves the map visible and opens its details. Molds without flight ratings have no map position, so the button is disabled with that reason.
- **Deselect.** Escape or a click on empty space closes the details the bag opened. Since Oct 6 the discs stay out; Escape returns focus to the disc. A click on the bag body that only dismisses does not also toggle the flap. With no details open, Escape leaves the top view.
- **Touch taps.** Taps are read from the pointer events, not the click. Chrome drops the click for the first tap after the turntable drag, which would otherwise make that tap do nothing. The click that follows a handled tap is swallowed, so it can't land on the panel that just opened.
- **Top view.** The "Top view" toggle beside Open bag moves the camera to straight above the putter pocket in 0.6 s (eased) and back on a second click or Escape. Since Oct 5 the bag stays opaque (no x-ray): the frame fits the top pocket, the go-to hides halfway up, and a soft mask (`--bag-top-fade`) fades the rest of the bag into the page, so only the putter pocket shows, labeled with its putters. Only putters are in reach from above. The user's turn is kept, and leaving the top view returns to the camera as it was.
- **Putters stow (Oct 5).** Closing the bag also sinks the putters into the top pocket, all at one height with only their rims above the mouth. Opening brings them back out into the same aligned row, front putter first, 70 ms apart, with a slight spring. The bag mounts closed, so the first reveal shows them rising. Stowed putters stay reachable (keyboard and their rims).
- **Putter pocket (Oct 6).** A "Putter pocket" button beside Top view (`[data-bag-pocket]`, `aria-expanded`) collapses the pocket: every putter slides down into its stowed seat with the same wave as closing the bag, while the flap stays open. A second press brings them back up into their row. If a putter is out, it slides home first (closing its details if they show it), then the row goes down. Other out discs stay out. The control is disabled while the bag is closed or has no putters. Mid-move it is `aria-disabled`, so keyboard focus stays on it. The choice holds through closing and opening the bag (`#bagScene[data-putter-pocket]`: open, moving, collapsed). Putters still come out of a collapsed pocket (click or the Putter type button) and go back into it.
- **Drag to spin (Oct 6).** A drag can start anywhere in the canvas box, discs and out-disc names included (the viewer's `dragSurface`; `touch-action: pan-y` on the discs too). A flick coasts and slows to a stop. Releasing after holding still stops at once, and the bag holds whatever angle it is left at. A press that moves under 8 px is still a tap, so the disc toggles. Pressing a coasting bag catches it, and that press is not a tap. Targets and labels stay hidden (`data-dragging`) until the coast ends. Reduced motion: no coast.
- **Hover glow (Oct 5).** Hovering or focusing a disc lights it in its own color (emissive, 160 ms); only one disc at a time, and none at rest.
- **Framing.** The page camera sits 3 cm higher (position y 0.20, target y 0.075), so a putter slid out of the top pocket stays inside the canvas.

## Out discs in side columns (Oct 6–7 2026)

- **Toggle.** A click, tap or Enter slides a disc out and opens its details. The disc stays out until the same disc is clicked again, through Escape, empty-space clicks, closing the bag, the top view, redraws and leaving the page. Each disc is independent. Out discs read as pressed (`aria-pressed`), and their label says Enter puts them back. An out main disc stays in reach with the flap shut. In the top view only putters are in reach, as before.
- **Side columns, any count (Oct 7).** Map mode is gone: however many discs are out, they rest in columns beside the bag. The split is relative (`assignSides`): ranked by stability among the out discs, the less overstable half goes left and the more overstable half right, so an all-overstable set still splits evenly. With an odd count the median disc joins the neighbor it is nearer in stability (the right on a tie, and a lone disc rests on the right). Faster discs sit higher in each column, and the rows line up across the bag. `sideFrame` in `bag-layout.mjs` lays them out. Each side starts as one column hugging the bag's visible silhouette (`SIDE_BAG`: x ±.235, y −.28 to .40). As it grows, its rows close up, then names move beside the discs, then the discs get smaller (scale .58 down to .40). Only then does a side wrap into a second column beside the first (rows by speed, the more extreme disc of a row further out). The camera pulls back only when none of that fits.
- **Names on every out disc (Oct 7).** Every out disc is named in every layout and theme. There are no compact or hidden names any more. A layout puts all its names one way, the first that fits: centered under the discs (`data-names="below"`), under them flush with the disc's inner edge so they reach only outward (`flush`, for narrow sides), or beside the discs away from the bag (`outer`), so a tall column reads like a legend. On phones (≤700 px) the pill is one size smaller (11 px, at most 112 px wide). The map grid and its "More turn / Stronger fade" captions are gone from the bag. The Stitch grid under the main atlas stays. My Map is untouched.
- **Wide canvas (Oct 6).** With any disc out, the canvas spans the page's free width at its usual height (`wideCanvas` in `bag-scene.js`: `data-wide` and the `--bag-wide-*` sizes). That runs from the left gutter (24 px, 8 on a phone) to the right gutter, or to the details panel while it shows beside the bag. The camera's vertical framing depends only on the height, so the bag keeps its size. When the panel opens or closes, the canvas's middle moves, and the bag slides there (0.38 s) rather than jumping. The out discs lay out again for the new width. Window resizes lay them out again too. With nothing out, the canvas goes back to its 4:5 box.
- **Planning on the real view.** The camera looks on from one side and above, so the canvas covers a skewed patch of the discs' plane. The viewer lays the canvas corners and the kept-clear boxes (zoom pill, pocket button) onto that plane and hands them to `sideFrame` (`view`, `center`, `keep`). Every layout the planner would take is then projected for real at its pull-back (`verify`). A layout that would touch an edge or a kept-clear box first draws its rows in a little, then gives way to the next preference. Results at 1440 px: the bag stays full size for every demo mix up to all eighteen out. One to eight have full-size discs with centered names, twelve form one legend column a side, and eighteen take two columns a side. At 360 px: about 1.8× for 1–8 out, 2.4× for twelve, and 3.4× for all eighteen as a legend.
- **Return all.** A pill at the canvas's top left, across from the zoom pill and as tall, shows "Return all" with the count while any disc is out. One click or Enter stows every out disc at once, each with its own slide-in, and closes the details the bag opened. The camera comes home and focus moves to the Open/Close bag button. Discs, names and the camera keep clear of the button the same way they keep clear of the zoom pill. In Stitch paint it is glass with a hairline edge, and the count is a lime mono badge.
- **Type buttons (Oct 6).** Next to the Sort discs control, Distance, Fairway, Midrange and Putter each pull out every disc of that type, typed as the Atlas types it (`typeOf`). A second click puts them all back, and discs out on their own stay out. A disc already out counts toward its type. A button is pressed while all its discs are out and disabled when the bag has none. A closed bag opens first, and the top view closes. Return all releases every type. They do not open details. Sorting saves to the account, so the signed-out demo bag hides the sort control but keeps the type buttons.
- **Camera.** The bag stays at its page framing whenever the columns fit beside it, which is every desktop case above. The camera pulls straight back only when they cannot fit (phones, or a large crowd beside an open details panel), as little as the real projection allows. It comes back in when discs go home.
- **Transitions.** Leaving its pocket keeps the slide's own feel. The disc then travels to its spot (about 0.5 s), and a path that would cross the bag bows out in front of it. When a disc comes out or goes home, every other out disc glides to its new spot in 0.65 s while the camera eases to its new pull-back, so nothing pops. Names fade while discs move. Out discs hold still in the room while the bag turns under a drag. Reduced motion stages instantly.
- **Main atlas grid.** The same Stitch grid (48 px, neutral `--stitch-grid`, luminance only) sits under the atlas map in light, midnight and charcoal, as `#map::before` beneath the canvas and markers. Markers, labels and grouping are unchanged.
- **Toolbar.** The map dropdown's "My discs" is now "My Bag" (also in the ☰ menu's Default map). The Brand chip is now "Manufacturer" and leads the chip row before Driver. It shows the manufacturer's name, or "2 manufacturers" when two are picked, and its panel and the Filters drawer say manufacturers too.
- **Tests.** `tests/bag-3d-layout.mjs` unit-tests the staging math. It checks that there is no map mode at any count. It checks the relative split at every count from 1 to 24 for mixed, all-overstable, all-understable and neutral sets: even, exact at the median for even counts, the median-neighbor rule for odd ones, and a single disc on the right. For `sideFrame` it checks side columns at desktop counts up to 24 at full size: each side's first column hugging the bag, faster discs higher, nothing on the bag, a kept-clear box or another disc or name, everything inside the frame, and the same layout every time. It also covers growth before wrapping (one column a side up to fourteen), the panel strip, phones pulling back with names kept (never further for fewer discs), flush names and crowds past every layout. `tests/bag-staging-browser.mjs` (port 8807, the signed-out demo bag, `outputs/bag-staging/`) checks the drawn layout from a single disc up to all eighteen. It steps through an overstable-leaning eight one disc at a time (0/1 … 4/4) and covers six stability mixes at 1440 and 360 px. It checks every name shown in full and clear of the bag, discs, names, zoom pill, pocket button and return button, in midnight, light and charcoal. It also covers the 5↔6 glides, return all, type buttons, the open details panel, reduced motion, the toolbar labels and the atlas grid. Screenshots: `outputs/bag-staging/screenshots.html`.

## Performance

- Rendering is on demand. The loop runs only during motion and the gentle float (throttled to 30 fps when nothing else moves). It stops when the bag is offscreen or the tab is hidden. Shaders compile up front with `compileAsync`. Mount phases yield to the main thread.
- **Phone profile** (360×800, DPR 2.625, 4× CPU throttling via CDP, hardware GPU): first open 1.5 s; frame p95 is 16.8 ms idle, 16.8 ms for close/open and 16.7 ms for a lift sweep across every disc. That is zero frames over 34 ms and no main-thread task over 100 ms during interaction. Loading the bag the first time costs three main-thread tasks (95 / 212 / 112 ms at 4×). Most of that is three.js module evaluation and model upload, shown under "Loading your bag…".
- **Caveat:** the GPU was a desktop RTX 3070, so this profile proves main-thread headroom, not mobile GPU fill rate. No physical phone was tested.
- **Software WebGL** (SwiftShader, llvmpipe, blocklisted GPUs) is detected automatically. The viewer then drops MSAA, caps the pixel ratio at 1 and stops ambient motion. It draws motion at half resolution and settles on one full-resolution frame. Under SwiftShader the existing scene suite measures a 23.7 ms average frame cadence during lifts, and the open completes inside its 1 s budget.

Large disc art is 62 px (was 54).
- **Your bag on the atlas.** Signed in with a bag, a three-way control beside the search (`#atlasLens`, a radio group) chooses **My bag** (default): bag discs wear their bag colors (first copy of a mold wins; Storage discs excluded), carry a ring and a "Your bag" legend entry, lead their flight groups and claim labels first (`atlasPriority()` puts them ahead of `featured.js`), and show even outside the Current + recent collection; **Default**: the shared atlas; **Only my discs**: just the bag, reframed at 1x. The choice is kept in `localStorage` (`atlas-lens`). `my-bag.js` publishes the colors (`BagApp.mapColors()`) and fires `atlas-bag-change` when they change. The personal bag stays a later plan.

## Pocket arrangement (correction)

Putters ride in the **top** pocket and the go-to disc sits in the **front** pocket above the main compartment, matching a real bag. The first version had these reversed. `bag-layout.mjs` now defines the pockets by location (`TOP`, `FRONT`) and maps `PUTTER` to the top and `GOTO` to the front. The viewer carries the GLB's go-to accent rim and dashed outline from the top pocket, where the model authors them, to the go-to's front seat. Screenshots of putters only, go-to only, both, and an empty go-to, at desktop and 360 px: `outputs/bag-3d/pocket-check/`.
