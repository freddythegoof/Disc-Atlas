About Disc Atlas

Updated September 24, 2026 · Invited preview

Disc Atlas helps players explore disc flight ratings, compare molds and see how their own bag covers different shots.

What the map tells you

Speed is plotted vertically. The provisional stability index runs horizontally: 50 + 10 × (turn + fade), capped between 0 and 100. The four flight numbers remain visible because the index alone cannot describe a disc's full flight.

How we present products

Model names are linked to a dated approval-record snapshot. Ratings and availability evidence are cited in each disc's details. The artwork is an original generic illustration, clearly labeled rather than presented as an actual product photograph.

Independent recommendations

Bag coverage is based on the discs you save and your optional playing profile. Retailer commissions do not affect the current fitting algorithm. The site does not sell discs or process purchases.

Preview status

The site is still an invited preview while source-data permissions, business/contact details and public-launch policies are reviewed. A private preview is not a substitute for data licensing. No commercial data-reuse permission or affiliation is implied.
