Privacy notice

Updated October 3, 2026 · Invited preview

This notice describes the current invited preview. A public operator/contact address and provider-retention review are still being finalized. For questions during this preview, contact the person who invited you. Remove individual discs in My Bag or delete your Atlas account in Account settings.

Information the application uses

Sign-in on the public Atlas is handled through Google. Disc Atlas stores your Google account identifier, email address and display name, along with a private session. The account identifier links your saved information; Atlas does not maintain a separate password database.

My Bag stores your bag model and capacity, plus one record per physical disc: catalog mold, plastic, wear from 1–10, weight in grams, optional notes and when it was added. Avoid putting sensitive information in notes.

Why and where it is used

This information keeps your bag with your Google-backed Atlas account. Bag records use Cloudflare D1 and are checked against the signed-in account for every read and update. Other visitors cannot browse your saved bag through the app. V1 does not use your bag for a personal map or coach recommendations. Authorized service operators and infrastructure providers may process records to operate and troubleshoot the service.

Atlas Coach

Atlas Coach requires Google sign-in. Sending a question transmits the included conversation and relevant Atlas catalog context to Cloudflare Workers AI, or to OpenAI when the alternate service is configured. Your email, name, saved bag, profile and photos are not sent as coach context.

Chat stays in this page’s memory. Conversations are not stored in the application database. Daily message counts are linked to your account in D1 to enforce the 20-message allowance, resetting at midnight UTC. A separate monthly cost ledger tracks OpenAI usage and reservations without chat content or account identity. OpenAI requests use storage disabled; provider security and abuse-monitoring records may still be retained under provider policies.

Clear chat removes the conversation from the page; signing out also clears it. Deleting your Google-backed Atlas account removes its daily coach counts. Monthly aggregate spend records remain to enforce the service budget. You can browse the Atlas without using the coach.

Browser storage and retailer links

The application stores your selected light, midnight or charcoal theme in your browser. No guest bag is stored in the browser. Authentication and hosting may use necessary cookies or logs. The current application code includes no advertising pixels or third-party analytics scripts, and does not record retailer clicks. Fonts and catalog illustrations are served locally.

Opening a retailer link takes you to another site's privacy policy. Retailers can collect visit information and may use cookies. When affiliate tracking is activated, identified links may carry a retailer-issued referral identifier. Disc Atlas does not take payment or receive your retailer account or card details.

Your controls and retention

You can edit your bag model and capacity, edit each physical disc or remove it in My Bag. Deleting your Google-backed Atlas account in Account settings deletes its bag and physical-disc records and revokes every session.

Application records remain until you remove them or delete your Atlas account. Account deletion removes your bag, physical-disc records and daily coach-usage records from active application storage. It does not delete your Google account, information held by retailers, provider logs or backups. Provider backup and log retention periods are not yet verified; no immediate-erasure promise is made for those systems.

For privacy questions or requests not covered by these controls, use your existing invitation contact during the preview. A monitored public privacy/contact address is required before public launch.
