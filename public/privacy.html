Privacy notice

Updated September 24, 2026 · Invited preview

This notice describes the current invited preview. A public operator/contact address and provider-retention review are still being finalized. For questions during this preview, contact the person who invited you. Self-service export and deletion are available under My bag → Your data.

Information the application uses

Sign-in is handled through ChatGPT. Disc Atlas receives an account identifier, email address, and name when supplied. The account identifier links your saved information; the application displays your email and name but does not maintain a separate password database.

If you save them, we store your throwing profile (skill, hand/style, course preferences, goals, distance, release speed and RPM), bag contents, plastic, weight, wear, colors, notes, and uploaded disc photos. Measurements are optional. Avoid putting sensitive information in notes or photos.

Why and where it is used

This information supports saved bags, personal maps, and recommendations. Application records and uploaded photos use the site's hosted database and object storage. Photo requests and saved-data access are checked against the signed-in account. Other visitors cannot browse your saved bag through the app. Authorized service operators and infrastructure providers may process data to operate and troubleshoot the service.

Atlas Coach

Atlas Coach requires Google sign-in. Sending a question transmits the included conversation and relevant Atlas catalog context to Cloudflare Workers AI, or to OpenAI when the alternate service is configured. Your email, name, saved bag, profile and photos are not sent as coach context.

Chat stays in this page’s memory. Conversations are not stored in the application database. Daily message counts are linked to your account in D1 to enforce the 20-message allowance, resetting at midnight UTC. A separate monthly cost ledger tracks OpenAI usage and reservations without chat content or account identity. OpenAI requests use storage disabled; provider security and abuse-monitoring records may still be retained under provider policies.

Clear chat removes the conversation from the page; signing out also clears it. Deleting your Google-backed Atlas account removes its daily coach counts. Monthly aggregate spend records remain to enforce the service budget. You can browse the Atlas without using the coach.

Browser storage and retailer links

The application stores your selected light/dark theme in your browser. Authentication and hosting may use necessary cookies or logs. The current application code includes no advertising pixels or third-party analytics scripts, and does not record retailer clicks. Fonts and catalog illustrations are served locally.

Opening a retailer link takes you to another site's privacy policy. Retailers can collect visit information and may use cookies. When affiliate tracking is activated, identified links may carry a retailer-issued referral identifier. Disc Atlas does not take payment or receive your retailer account or card details.

Your controls and retention

You can edit your profile, remove individual discs/photos, export your profile and bag as JSON, download your personal photos separately, and delete all saved Disc Atlas data. Use My bag → Your data. The export does not contain photo bytes; download them before deletion.

Application records remain until you remove them or use full deletion. Full deletion removes your profile, bag records, referenced uploaded photos and coach-usage records from active application storage. It does not delete your ChatGPT account, information held by retailers, provider logs or backups. Provider backup and log retention periods are not yet verified; no immediate-erasure promise is made for those systems.

For privacy questions or requests not covered by these controls, use your existing invitation contact during the preview. A monitored public privacy/contact address is required before public launch.
