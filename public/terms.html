Use & disclosures

Updated September 24, 2026 · Invited preview

Invited-preview guidelines. Final public business details and legal terms are pending review. These guidelines do not claim that the site or its source data has received legal clearance.

An independent reference

Disc Atlas is an independent disc-golf reference and bag-planning tool. It is not operated, approved, or sponsored by the PDGA, disc manufacturers, retailers, or professional players unless a specific relationship is expressly stated. Brand and model names identify the products being discussed; their appearance is not an endorsement.

Ratings, illustrations and availability

The catalog is a dated snapshot. Flight ratings and production-status matches may be incomplete or incorrect. The shared stability index and flight paths are illustrative comparisons, not measured predictions or a PDGA rating. Confirm equipment approval and competition rules with the current official sources.

Disc images are generic original AI-generated artwork with model labels. They do not show actual stock, exact mold geometry, plastic, color, stamp, or condition. Uploaded photos in your private bag are your own submitted images.

Shopping and commissions

Retailer links currently earn no affiliate commission. If approved tracking is activated, a commission disclosure will appear beside the shopping links and tracked links will be labeled. Retailers handle stock, prices, shipping, payment, returns, and customer service. A catalog or search link does not establish availability.

Bag suggestions use saved playing information and catalog criteria. The current recommendation algorithm does not use commission rates or affiliate status to rank discs. Suggestions are optional ideas for field testing; one disc can cover several shots.

Your uploads and account

Upload only images you own or have permission to use. Do not upload another person's sensitive information, abusive or unlawful content. By uploading, you permit the service to store, resize, and display that image to you as needed for your bag. This does not give Disc Atlas permission to sell your photo or turn it into a public catalog image.

Keep your sign-in account secure. Do not attempt to access others' data, interfere with the service, or circumvent usage limits. You can remove your uploads and saved records using the data controls.

AI and practice

Atlas Coach, when activated, is an AI assistant and can make mistakes. It does not represent a professional player or provide guaranteed performance. Use a safe practice area and stop throwing if it causes pain.

Questions and corrections

During this invited preview, contact the person who invited you for corrections, rights concerns or service issues. A monitored public contact channel is being prepared before launch.
