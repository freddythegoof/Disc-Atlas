A COMMON LANGUAGE, WITH LIMITS

The shared scale.

Every rated disc uses the same transparent stability index: 50 + 10 × (turn + fade), capped at 0–100. Lower means more understable; higher means more overstable. Neutral spans 40–60.

Discs are scattered slightly around their published flight coordinates for readability. These display offsets do not represent measured flight differences. Nearby discs group together when space is limited; choose Explore nearby discs in the detail panel to see every member and its exact ratings. Zoom in to explore more detail. This is a provisional comparison index, not a measured cross-brand calibration. Reformatting ratings cannot remove manufacturers’ different testing assumptions. Two discs with the same index can have very different turn and fade; always compare all four numbers and the path.

Flight numbers come from the DiscIt / Marshall Street catalog, supplemented by reviewed manufacturer model matches. PDGA records establish approval, not flight ratings. Exact name-and-brand matches are supplemented by explicit, sourced mappings to specific PDGA approvals (for example, DD3 to DD3 (new)). Older approvals are kept separate. Unmatched molds remain in the directory without invented ratings.

Paths illustrate a flat release in calm conditions. The power control changes the sketch, not a verified distance prediction. Plastic, weight, wear, release, spin and wind can change the actual flight. Catalog artwork uses an original generic disc illustration with plain-text model labels. It does not reproduce manufacturer stamps or exact mold geometry.

Current + recent collection

The default view is a catalog-based approximation of current availability, not a verified inventory of every active mold. It includes discs in the retrieved DiscIt / Marshall Street catalog plus molds with documented retirement announcements within the preceding 24 months. Documented older retirements and unverified records are available under Show everything. A missing status never means a disc is definitely discontinued.

The window is anchored to the data snapshot, September 23, 2026: announcements on or after September 23, 2024 are included. Announcement dates stand in for retirement dates where the final production date is not published. Remaining stock and limited reissues may exist. Approval dates, flight ratings, and photo availability are never used as proof of active production.

Production-status coverage is incomplete; catalog listings can include older stock. Each detail panel links to its status evidence. Original and retooled models keep their separate records.
